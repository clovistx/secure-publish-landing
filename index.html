The problem

AI generates panels.
Sharing still leaks.

Models spit out dashboards, static sites, and analyses every day. Teams still drop them into Drive, Notion, or a public URL — and the share path becomes the leak.

Drive & Notion sprawl

HTML panels become attachments or weak previews. Permissions drift. Reviewers can’t tell what’s current or who can open it.

Public links leak

Anyone with the link can view the panel. A Slack forward is enough — the URL alone is the leak.

AI panels need a gate

Access or VPN covers internal apps. Here the job is fast CLI publish of the HTML the AI just generated — as a gated page, not a loose file.

The product

CLI publish. Edge SSO gate.
Share only with signed-in users.

Secure Publish wraps your HTML panel so authentication is checked before content is served. No loose public file for unauthenticated visitors.

01

Publish with the CLI

Point the CLI at your AI-generated HTML panel and ship it in one command. Proof anchor: panel-vendas — Painel Vendas Q3.

$ panel-gate publish ./painel-vendas-q3.html --title "Painel Vendas Q3"
02

SSO gate at the edge

Requests without a valid company session are refused before HTML is served. The URL alone is not enough.

03

Share with your team

Send the panel URL to colleagues. Authenticated users see the dashboard; everyone else hits the gate — not a public file.
